# lowkey 0.1.3

## Shared BobukKit 0.5.0 color themes

- **18 built-in palettes:** expanded Appearance presets to 18 light and dark themes (adding Kanagawa, Everforest, Flexoki, Ayu, Monokai, and GitHub alongside Amber, Sage, Ocean, Terracotta, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Rosé Pine, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Solarized, and One Light / One Dark), with palette swatches next to the picker.
- **Full 10-color customization:** edit **Panel**, **Accent**, and **Text** directly or expand **More colors** for **Surface**, **Muted text**, **Border**, **Selection**, **Link**, **Success**, and **Danger** with per-role **Auto** contrast reset, side-by-side strip & palette preview, and WCAG contrast warnings.
- **Tint, Page, System, Light, and Dark modes** keep working as before, and existing `~/.config/lowkey/config.json` palettes remain fully compatible.

## Address prompt settings gear

- The `⌘L` address prompt footer now includes a settings gear button on the bottom-right to open lowkey Settings directly, and uses the theme's `selection` and `danger` tokens.

## Distribution

Universal macOS ZIP for Apple Silicon and Intel, Developer ID signed, notarized and stapled, with SHA-256 checksums. No automatic updater or Homebrew support.
